What it is

Your Friend, an old-school hero

Everything you'd expect from a classic browser RPG, drawn in the Rare Friends style: black one-bit sprites with white halos on a greyscale world with faded colour.

☺

Your Friend is the hero

The verified Generations NFT walks the Realm in its canonical on-chain sprite. Its family gives a perk: Hoverers run longer, Skeletons pray better, Colossi hit harder.

▦

Fifteen skills to 99

Combat, Magic and Prayer, plus Woodcutting, Fishing, Mining, Smithing, Cooking, Firemaking, Crafting, Thieving and Agility, on the classic XP curve.

☰

Right-click everything

Left-click does the first option, right-click lists them all: Chop down, Attack (level-5), Talk-to, Pickpocket, Examine. WASD walks, the minimap takes you anywhere.

✎

Six quests

From baking for the Realm Feast to quieting a crypt, gathering the shards of a lost Glimmer, and facing the Hollow King in his throne room.

♪

A soundtrack for every region

Sixteen procedural tracks, from Hollow Square to Anvil Song and the Hollow King's battle theme, plus level-up and quest fanfares. All synthesized live, no samples.

◆

Friends, relics and saves

Your other owned Friends follow you (+1–5% XP by generation). Rare Caskets (simulated RF) hold relics and wardrobe pieces. Progress saves per wallet.

Explore

A 240 × 240 tile island

Twelve regions and two dungeons, each with its own music: cobbled Friendhollow, the farms, Whisperwood's Grumblins, the Ashen Hills mine, the Emberforge, snowy Frostpeak, Glass Lake, the Pale Dunes and Oasis, Murkmire's crypt, and the Mossy Ruins above the Hollow Depths.

    Rare Caskets

    Simulated through the SDK's reviewed chance-game client. No real RF moves.

    A casket costs 1 RF and returns 0.88 RF in expected value (12% stays with the game as prize stake). Each holds a Rare Relic, a bonus while you keep it or its RF value when you redeem it, plus a wardrobe piece drawn on your Friend.
